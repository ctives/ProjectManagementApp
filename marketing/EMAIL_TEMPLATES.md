## Email Sequence Strategy

### New User Onboarding Sequence
1. **Day 0**: Welcome email (sent immediately)
2. **Day 3**: Workflow examples
3. **Day 7**: Privacy deep dive
4. **Day 14**: Check-in / Feature tips
5. **Day 30**: Re-engagement (if inactive)

### Active User Engagement
- **Weekly**: Newsletter (only for users who opt in)
- **As needed**: Feature announcements
- **Monthly**: Survey/feedback request
- **Quarterly**: User story request

### Re-engagement for Inactive Users
- **Day 30**: First re-engagement
- **Day 60**: Second re-engagement (different angle)
- **Day 90**: Final re-engagement or unsubscribe suggestion

## Best Practices

### Subject Lines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Ask questions or create curiosity
- Avoid spam triggers (FREE, !!!, $$$)
- A/B test different approaches
- Personalize when possible

### Email Body
- Lead with value, not features
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Include clear call-to-action
- Make it scannable (bullets, headers, bold)
- Always include an easy unsubscribe option

### Timing
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 10 AM or 2 PM in recipient's timezone
- Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons
- Test and optimize based on your audience

### Personalization
- Use first name when available
- Segment based on behavior (active vs. inactive)
- Reference their specific usage (e.g., "We noticed you love custom columns")
- Tailor content to their use case when known

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate (aim for 20%+)
- Click-through rate (aim for 3%+)
- Reply rate (measure engagement)
- Unsubscribe rate (keep under 0.5%)
- Conversion to active use
